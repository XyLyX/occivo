# OCCIVO — Concierge Desk Card v1.1

**Internal quick reference · Keep alongside a live request**

### 1 · Triage (2 min)
**Deadline → Destination → All-in AED budget → Occasion/relationship/age → Avoid list → Real recipient words → Interests/presentation.**

**STOP & CLARIFY** if: country missing for international delivery; exact date matters; exclusions unclear; supplier cannot satisfy budget/quality/deadline. A deadline never overrides quality, safety or exclusions.

### 2 · Draft the trio (about 8 min)
- **Classic:** suitable, low taste risk, beautifully presented.
- **Personal:** grounded in an actual shared interest or user statement.
- **Memorable:** special but appropriate; *no unapproved above-budget upsell*.
- Vary type, emotional fit or presentation; three similar sets do not count.
- Write one **“why this person?”** sentence for every direction. If no recipient traits given, don't invent them; ask for one useful detail.

### 3 · Source & verify (time varies)
- [ ] Supplier, exact item/service and checked-at evidence
- [ ] Real item + Duboxx individual box + taxes + delivery + handling costs
- [ ] Avoidance/age/cultural and quality requirements passed
- [ ] Delivery destination and **exact** deadline/availability verified
- [ ] Packaging and handwritten card operationally feasible
- [ ] One backup where practical; no unverified third idea to reach three
- [ ] Quote margin = proposed revenue − direct costs (not net profit)

### 4 · Review, send, log (3–5 min after verification)
**Human approves** customer wording, evidence, all-in price and delivery caveats. Send from verified concierge address, log real send time, invite **“tell us what feels off”** refinement. Never treat an idea as a paid order.

**If sourcing is delayed:** send a truthful holding reply, with **no invented completion time**. Email receipts and SLA remain unverified until tested.

**20 minutes = editorial drafting ambition, NOT a sourcing or delivery guarantee.**
